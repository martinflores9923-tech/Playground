Overview

Protect your investment and keep your facility looking its best with professional commercial floor care services from Pacific Services Enterprises. High-traffic floors are one of the first things customers, employees, and visitors notice, and routine maintenance is essential to preserving their appearance, extending their lifespan, and maintaining a safe environment.

Our customized floor maintenance programs are designed for offices, medical facilities, schools, retail centers, warehouses, and commercial properties throughout Sonoma County. Whether you need routine maintenance or complete floor restoration, our experienced team uses professional equipment and proven techniques to deliver exceptional results.
