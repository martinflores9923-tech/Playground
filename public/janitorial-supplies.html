Overview

Keeping your facility fully stocked with essential restroom and janitorial supplies is just as important as keeping it clean. Pacific Services Enterprises offers convenient janitorial supply and consumable management services to ensure your business never runs out of the products your employees, customers, and visitors rely on every day.

As part of our commercial cleaning services or as a standalone facility support service, we monitor inventory, restock supplies, and help maintain a clean, fully supplied environment. Our team works with your preferred products or can recommend high-quality, cost-effective solutions based on your facility's needs.
