Overview

Construction may be complete, but the project isn't finished until the space is clean, safe, and ready for occupancy. Pacific Services Enterprises provides professional post-construction cleaning services for both commercial and residential projects throughout Sonoma County.

Whether you're a general contractor, builder, property manager, developer, or homeowner, our experienced team delivers detailed cleaning that removes construction dust, debris, adhesive residue, paint overspray, and other job-site contaminants. From rough cleaning during construction to meticulous final cleaning before occupancy, we ensure every project is presented at its absolute best.

Our Three-Phase Cleaning Process

Rough Clean

Removes construction debris, heavy dust, and job-site waste during or immediately after construction.

Final Clean

A detailed top-to-bottom cleaning that prepares the property for inspection, occupancy, or client turnover.

Touch-Up Clean

A final visit after punch-list work is complete to remove any remaining dust, fingerprints, footprints, or debris before the owner or tenant takes possession.

From large commercial developments to newly constructed homes, Pacific Services Enterprises delivers the final clean that leaves every space looking polished, professional, and move-in ready.
