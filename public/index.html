About Pacific Services Enterprises

We are a family-owned and operated commercial cleaning company based in Rohnert Park, California. Our roots go back to 1990, when our uncle Filiberto built the business with a simple commitment: deliver reliable, detail-oriented cleaning services businesses can trust.

When Filiberto retired in 2015, he passed the business down to our family. Today the company is run by Ramon and Rosario, along with their children Ivonne and Irvin. Many team members are family, and we treat every account with the same care and consistency we would give our own facility.

Over the years, we have built lasting client relationships through consistent service, clear communication, and dependable follow-through. We believe great service goes beyond cleaning, it means being a partner you can rely on.
